# The base construction sector group (pmg_base_building_construction_sector) is now the FOUNDATIONS group.
# Keys are kept from vanilla; names are changed in localization/replace/english.
# Every tier uses BUILDING MATERIALS (brick, stone, timber, lime and cement) plus, from Brick and Lime on, the metal of its era, bought
# from ironworks and steel mills as in history: cast- and wrought-iron columns, beams and roofs (Brick and Lime), steel rebar and
# frames (Concrete), steel piling, caissons and high-rise frames (Piles). Timber Footings stays metal-free and buys wood directly.
# Construction points: Foundations 1.5 / 4.5 / 7.5 / 10.5 plus Fittings (HMPS_13_construction_groups.txt); Site Machinery gives none.
# Each era's best combination matches vanilla: Timber + Bare Finish 2.0 (850 per point), Brick + Bare Finish 5.0 (660),
# Concrete + Windows and Pipes 10.0 (525), Piles + Electric Lifts 15.0 (487).
# Every higher Foundations method costs fewer goods per point than adding another level with the lower one (about 15% margin).
