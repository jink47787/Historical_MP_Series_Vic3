# The base construction sector group (pmg_base_building_construction_sector) is now the FOUNDATIONS group.
# Keys are kept from vanilla; names are changed in localization/replace/english.
# Every tier uses BUILDING MATERIALS (brick, stone, timber, lime and cement) plus, from Brick and Lime on, the metal of its era, bought
# from ironworks and steel mills as in history: cast- and wrought-iron columns, beams and roofs (Brick and Lime), steel rebar and
# frames (Concrete), steel piling, caissons and high-rise frames (Piles). Each metal input replaced building materials of equal value.
# Timber Footings stays metal-free so countries without iron can still build at the start.
# Construction points: Foundations + the DEFAULT Fittings (Bare Finish 0.5); Site Machinery only saves labour, it gives no points.
# add up to 2 / 4.5 / 9.5 / 14.5; Windows and Pipes or better fittings reach or pass vanilla's 2 / 5 / 10 / 15 (see HMPS_13_construction_groups.txt).
# Goods cost per construction point at the default methods matches vanilla's for each era (about 1000 / 720 / 540 / 527).
