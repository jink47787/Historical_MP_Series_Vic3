# The base construction sector group (pmg_base_building_construction_sector) is now the FOUNDATIONS group.
# Keys are kept from vanilla; names are changed in localization/replace/english.
# Every tier uses BUILDING MATERIALS (brick, stone, timber, lime and cement) 30 / 40 / 50 / 60 plus wood (timber framing, scaffolding
# and formwork) and, from Brick and Lime on, the metal of its era: cast- and wrought-iron columns, beams and roofs (Brick and Lime),
# steel rebar and frames (Concrete), steel piling, caissons and high-rise frames (Piles). Other inputs follow vanilla where possible.
# Construction points: Foundations 2.0 / 5.0 / 8.0 / 11.0 plus Fittings (HMPS_13_construction_groups.txt); Site Machinery gives none.
# Each era's best combination matches vanilla points at base prices: Timber + Bare Finish 2.0 (1200 per point, vanilla +20%),
# Brick + Bare Finish 5.0 (860, +19%), Concrete + Windows and Pipes 10.0 (675, +25%), Piles + Electric Lifts 15.0 (620, +18%).
# Era 3 is above and era 4 below +20% because vanilla era 4 is only 2% cheaper per point than era 3.
# At base prices every higher Foundations method is cheaper per point than the lower one with any Fittings method
# (margins 28% / 11-16% / 3-6%), so the newest methods are always best. Engines stand in for vanilla's electricity.
# Jobs: 5000 per level, the normal industry size (raised from vanilla 1500 on 2026-11-02 so construction wages match other industries).
