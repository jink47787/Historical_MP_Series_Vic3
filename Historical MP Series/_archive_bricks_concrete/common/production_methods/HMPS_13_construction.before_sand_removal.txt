# The base construction sector group (pmg_base_building_construction_sector) is now the FOUNDATIONS group.
# Keys are kept from vanilla; names are changed in localization/replace/english.
# Every tier is primarily BUILDING MATERIALS (70-80% of input value). The other inputs deliberately avoid every good the
# Builders' Yards consume (wood, coal, tools, electricity, sand, stone, steel), so the two do not compete.
# Construction points: Foundations + the DEFAULT Site Machinery (Manual Labour 1.0) and Fittings (Bare Finish 0.5)
# add up to vanilla's 2 / 5 / 10 / 15. Upgrading machinery or fittings adds on top (see HMPS_13_construction_groups.txt).
