# Builders' Yards (building_materials_works): two material lines (Timber, Masonry) that mirror each other, plus Handling and
# Standardisation add-ons. Each line carries about half of the building's 5000 jobs, so both lines together make the
# standard 5000 and the full output; value added per job follows the vanilla curve (see the VA audit).
# Every non-default method is unlocked by its own technology; no two methods share one.
